SWARMSYNTH —
click Start audio, or just play a key
Res Noise Pitch up · Pan across · Vol into the box

Play with A–L (and W E T Y U for the black keys), Z/X to change octave. Drag a knob vertically.

A recreation of SwarmSynth, a 2003 plugin by Anarchy Sound Software, rebuilt by measuring the original rather than disassembling it. Source and write-up.
